01. About Me

Hello! I'm Ashmina, a developer who enjoys building things that live on the internet. My interest in development started back in 2019 when I enrolled in a BSc Computing program at London Metropolitan University — turns out hacking together small websites taught me a lot about HTML & CSS.

Fast-forward to today, and I've had the privilege of working at an early-stage startup, a software studio, and a design institute — leading UI/UX work, shipping REST & GraphQL APIs, and mentoring interns along the way. My main focus these days is building accessible, human-centered products at Avila University while finishing my Master's in Computer Science.
